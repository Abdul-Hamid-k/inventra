# Varnix

**Billing, stock and GST for Indian shops — works offline, on Windows and Android.**

Point of sale with barcode scanning and loose goods by weight, GST invoices and GSTR-1 summary, stock and purchase orders on WhatsApp, customers with credit (udhaar), advance and WhatsApp reminders, smart reorder alerts, daily reports, staff PINs with approvals, computer and phone in sync, encrypted backups — in English, हिन्दी, मराठी, ગુજરાતી, தமிழ் and తెలుగు.
